What Are The Labors?

They are as follows:

  1. Nemean Lion
    1. Herakles was sent to Nemea to slay the lion with golden fur that was impervious to attack.
  2. Lernaean Hydra
    1. Herakles was sent to Lake Lerna to slay the hydra that dwelled in the lake. He called on his nephew for help.
  3. Ceryneian Hind
    1. King Eurystheus sent Herakles to Ceryneia to capture the Ceryneian Hind, a sacred animal to the goddess Artemis.
  4. Erymanthian Boar
    1. Next, Herakles was sentenced to bring the Erymanthian Boar back to king Eurystheus alive.
  5. Augean Stables
    1. The next task was for Herakles to clean the stables of the immortal cattle by himself. He rerouted the rivers Alpheus and Peneus to wash away the dung.
  6. Stymphalian Birds
    1. Herakles then was forced to slay the Symphalian birds, which were man-eating birds with bronze beaks and metal feathers. He shook a rattle and scared them into the air where he shot them with arrows.
  7. Cretan Bull
    1. King Eurystheus then instructed Herakles to sail to Crete and capture the Cretan Bull—the father of the Minotaur.
  8. Mares of Diomedes
    1. The next task was for Herakles to capture the man-eating mares belonging to Diomedes.
  9. Belt of Hippolyta
    1. Herakles then sailed to the land of the Amazons to return with the Belt of Hippolyta, the Queen of the Amazons.
  10. Cattle of Geryon
    1. The tenth task was to capture and bring back the cattle of the three-bodied giant Geryon.
  11. Golden Apples of the Hesperides
    1. The next task was to go to the garden of the Hesperides and return with three golden apples from Hera's sacred tree.
  12. Cerberus
    1. The final task was to venture into the underworld and capture the three-headed dog, and guardian of the gates of Hades, Cerberus.

WAIT!

Why are there twelve labors listed? I thought Herakles only had to complete ten labors?

The two additional labors are because King Eurystheus said Herakles had help during the second and fifth labors. In enlisting his nephew for help in the second labor, Herakles did not complete the labor alone, and by using the river to clean the stables in the fifth labor, Herakles did not complete this task by himself either.
