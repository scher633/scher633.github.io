Who was Herakles?

The son of Zeus and Alcmene, Herakles is half-mortal half-god. He was hated by the Queen of the Gods, Hera, for being born of one of Zeus's affairs. His life was forever changed by the goddess's hatred of him.

Growing up and becoming the strongest hero Greece had ever seen, Hera was enraged. Traditions of the story differ, but according to Apollodorus's book the Bibliotecha, Herakles comes home in a state of madness and mistaking his wife and children for intruders, kills them.

In Ancient Greek Culture, killing one's own family was one of the worst crimes a person can commit. As resititution, Herakles was sentenced to servitude under the king of Tiryns, Eurystheus. Herakles was promised forgiveness and immortality if he completed ten labors in ten years under Eurystheus.
